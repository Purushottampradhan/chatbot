🤖 Chatbot Widget Demo

This is a demonstration of the embeddable chatbot widget. The chat widget will appear in the bottom right corner.

Integration Example

<div id="chatbot-widget"></div>
<script src="chatbot-widget.js"></script>
<script>
  ChatbotWidget.mount({
    containerId: 'chatbot-widget',
    socketUrl: 'http://localhost:5001',
    userId: 'user123',
    title: 'Support Chat',
    theme: 'light'
  });
</script>

Try clicking the chat button and ask questions about company policies or general inquiries!
